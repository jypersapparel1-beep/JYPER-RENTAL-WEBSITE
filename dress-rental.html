YOUR JYPER DRESS RENTAL GUIDE

Dress Pick-up & Return Options

Choose the option that works best for you when collecting and returning your dress rental in General Santos City.

👗

Visit Our Fitting Location

We recommend visiting our fitting location first so you can try on the dress and make sure it fits comfortably before renting.

View Fitting Location & Google Maps Directions ↗
📦

Box / Shop Pick-up & Return

You may collect and return your dress at our Box (Orange Building), in front of and beside Gaisano Mall and KCC Gensan.

View Box / Shop Photo & Google Maps Directions ↗
📍

Meet-up Areas

Meet-up arrangements are available in Tambler, Uhaw, and Calumpang. Please coordinate the schedule with us in advance.

🛵

Delivery via Maxim

Maxim delivery is available if you are already sure that the dress fits your size and you do not need to try it on first. Delivery and return fees are shouldered by the renter.

Important fitting reminder: If you would like to try on the dress before renting, please visit our fitting location. Maxim delivery is only for renters who are confident that the selected dress is the correct fit and no fitting or try-on is needed. You may also choose Box / shop drop-off or arrange a meet-up in Tambler, Uhaw, or Calumpang.
